Blocking on your phone

This page is a synced dashboard — it can't block sites on its own; a web page fundamentally can't intercept other apps' traffic. Real system-wide blocking on Android takes two minutes with a built-in setting:

  1. Open your phone's Settings → Network & internet → Private DNS
  2. Choose "Private DNS provider hostname"
  3. Enter: family-filter-dns.cleanbrowsing.org
  4. Save

This blocks adult content across every app and browser on your phone, not just one — and it's free, with no app to install or maintain.

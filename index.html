          Blocking on your phone

          This app is a synced dashboard — it can't block sites on its own; a web page fundamentally can't intercept other apps' traffic. Real system-wide blocking on Android takes two minutes with a built-in setting:

          1. Open your phone's Settings → Network & internet → Private DNS
          2. Choose "Private DNS provider hostname"
          3. Enter: family-filter-dns.cleanbrowsing.org
          4. Save

          This is deliberately a few steps, not a one-tap toggle — that's a feature, not friction to fix. It means removing it mid-urge takes real deliberate effort.

          What this app can't do

          No live notifications from this app (that needs a background server this design intentionally doesn't have — notifications come from the extension on your computer, or nothing on mobile). No two-way sync — nothing here writes back to the extension. No site blocking from the app itself — see above.
